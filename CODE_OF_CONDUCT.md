# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want Kinthic to be an open-source project where people can collaborate on ambitious ideas without hostility, contempt, or exclusion.

Please:

- assume good faith
- critique ideas and code, not people
- keep feedback specific and actionable
- avoid harassment, dogpiling, and personal attacks
- respect privacy and security concerns

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- hateful or discriminatory language
- doxxing or sharing private information
- deliberately unsafe guidance intended to harm users
- persistent bad-faith disruption of discussions or reviews

## Scope

This applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and community spaces associated with Kinthic.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or reject content that violates this standard and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
