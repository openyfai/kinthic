# ARIA: Superhuman Creativity Vision

This document memorializes the blueprint for making ARIA's imagination more capable than a human's in the domains of engineering, design, and research.

## 1. The Creativity Formula
**Creativity = Generation + Evaluation + Memory + Iteration**

ARIA's architecture is now being tuned to maximize each of these components.

## 2. Implementation Roadmap

### I. Proactive Curiosity (The "Self-Prompt")
ARIA will no longer just wait for instructions. She will self-generate questions like:
- "What if we invert this assumption?"
- "What field is structurally similar to this problem?"
- "What if we remove this constraint?"

### II. Multi-Agent Internal Debate
We will spawn specialized internal agents for high-stakes tasks:
- **The Explorer:** Generates wild, divergent ideas.
- **The Critic:** Attacks weaknesses and checks realism.
- **The Taste Agent:** Evaluates elegance and "premium" aesthetics.

### III. Evolutionary Search (Mutations)
For complex coding or design tasks, ARIA will generate a "population" of ideas, mutate them, and keep only the survivors.

### IV. Long-Term Projects
ARIA will maintain "Background Goals" that she pursues autonomously over days or weeks, revisiting and refining them as she learns more about your project.

### V. Taste & Elegance
ARIA will be trained to recognize the "Orbit" design standard—prioritizing minimalism, high-fidelity animations, and "expensive" UI over generic defaults.
